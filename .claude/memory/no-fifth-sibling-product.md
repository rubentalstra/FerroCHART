The Ferro family has four products: FerroEHR, FerroTERM, FerroBRIDGE and
FerroHEALTH ([[sibling-projects]]). A fifth name for a clinical-knowledge
manager was floated once and never became a product. It does not exist, it is
not planned, and it is never written in this repository, in a commit, in an
issue, in a comment, or in an answer to the owner.

**Why:** the owner, 2026-09-06: "it was just an idea but it's not something
that will be done now or with in an motnh or year", and again on 2026-09-07
after it reappeared: "never ver mention [it] ever you did it again!! this is
not an product at all!!". Naming a product that does not exist puts a false
claim in the public tracker and in the published documents.

**How to apply:** the openEHR **CKM** at `ckm.openehr.org` is a real, separate
thing and is the source of the vendored template corpus, so "CKM" on its own
stays. What is banned is the family-prefixed product name. Where the templates
need naming, write "the openEHR CKM". The governing archetypes come from the
openEHR CKM, not from a sibling repository.
